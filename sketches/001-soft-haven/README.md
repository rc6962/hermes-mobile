## Variant: Soft Haven

### Design stance
A calm private workspace that makes an advanced assistant feel safe and understandable to ordinary users.

### Key choices
- Layout: balanced chat-first surface with a compact session rail.
- Typography: large friendly headings and restrained body text.
- Color: paper background, indigo ink, lavender-blue action accents.
- Interaction: status card expands to reveal safe activity details.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: onboarding confidence, readability, consumer approachability.
- Weak at: fitting many tools or dense operational data on one screen.

### Best for
A Play Store audience that wants a capable assistant without a developer-console appearance.
