## Variant: Sunlit Companion

### Design stance
A warm, human, everyday assistant with visible privacy and runtime readiness without technical jargon.

### Key choices
- Layout: chat-first with a compact context/status header and bottom action rail.
- Typography: rounded, conversational scale with clear hierarchy.
- Color: cream, apricot, coral, and deep plum.
- Interaction: the status card toggles between “ready” and “details”.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: friendliness, consumer onboarding, visual distinctiveness.
- Weak at: power-user workflows and dark-room use.

### Best for
A monetized consumer product positioned as a personal assistant rather than a technical bridge.
